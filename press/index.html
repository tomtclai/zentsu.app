01 · METHOD

One window: a drop zone, a queue, and the output picker.

Output lands beside the source file with a (Press) suffix. The original is never touched, and Press asks once for access to the folder so the sandbox allows the write.

Video

H.264 MP4 that plays where HEVC was refused.

Finder's Encode action defaults to HEVC and QuickTime Player exports only .mov, which is why a clip that plays on your Mac gets bounced by a Windows PC or an upload form. Press writes .mp4 with H.264 video and AAC audio at Original, 1080p, or 720p, and copies the streams without re-encoding when the source is already H.264.

Press Pro adds 2160p, HEVC output for when you want the smaller file, and size presets: Discord at 20 MB, Email at 25 MB, Slack at 1 GB, or a custom number. Press works out the bitrate from the byte budget and the clip's length, keeps the full duration, and re-encodes once at a lower rate if the first pass overshoots.

Image

WebP out, which Finder and Preview cannot do.

Finder's Convert Image writes JPEG, PNG, or HEIF; Preview's Export adds TIFF and PDF. Press reads HEIC, HEIF, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, GIF, and BMP through ImageIO and writes JPEG, PNG, and HEIC with a quality slider.

Press Pro adds WebP (lossless at 100 percent, through libwebp), AVIF, TIFF, and a Max size in pixels. Orientation is applied to the pixels before encoding, so a phone photo comes out upright everywhere.

02 · FORMATS

What goes in, what comes out.

Anything AVFoundation opens as video, anything ImageIO decodes as an image. MKV, AVI, audio, and documents are outside this release.

Video in

MOV, MP4, M4V, and the other QuickTime-family files, including HEVC and ProRes sources from an iPhone or a camera.

Video out

MP4 with H.264 or HEVC video and AAC audio at Original, 720p, 1080p, or 2160p. Sources are never scaled up. Size presets for Discord, email, and Slack, or a custom limit. HEVC, 2160p, and size presets are Press Pro.

Images

In: HEIC, HEIF, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, GIF, BMP. Out: JPEG, PNG, and HEIC free; WebP, AVIF, and TIFF with Press Pro. Quality from 10 to 100 percent where the format is lossy, and an optional max pixel size.

05 · FAQ

Questions before you download.

If yours isn't here, email support@zentsu.app. Every message is read by a human.

Does Press upload my files anywhere?

No. Conversion runs through AVFoundation, ImageIO, and libwebp on your Mac. The source has no networking code in it; the only network traffic is the App Store purchase and restore flow, which macOS handles with Apple.

Why does the MP4 from Finder still get rejected?

Finder's Encode Selected Video Files defaults to HEVC unless you tick Greater compatibility, and QuickTime Player's Export As writes only .mov. An HEVC file is what an older Windows machine or an upload form refuses. Press writes H.264 in an .mp4, which is the combination those systems expect.

What is free, and what needs Press Pro?

Free: one file at a time to H.264 MP4 at Original, 1080p, or 720p, and to JPEG, PNG, or HEIC, with no cap on how many times. Pro: batch conversion, size presets, 2160p, HEVC output, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, and image downscaling, as a one-time ${{ site.price_press_lifetime }} purchase.

Does it change my original files?

Never. Press writes name (Press).mp4 or name (Press).jpg beside the source, adds a number if that name already exists, and asks once for access to the folder so the sandbox allows the write.

What happens to metadata?

Video: AVFoundation carries the source's metadata into the MP4 wherever the container has a matching field, except on size-limited exports, which are written fresh through AVAssetWriter and carry none. Images: EXIF, including location, is not copied; the orientation tag is applied to the pixels first, so the picture displays upright.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel, as a universal binary from the Mac App Store.

How do I restore Press Pro on another Mac?

Open Press, go to Settings, and click Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. You can request a refund from Apple directly via reportaproblem.apple.com. If a conversion is failing, email support@zentsu.app first. There is a good chance it is a fixable bug.
