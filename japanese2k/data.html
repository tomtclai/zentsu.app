JP 2K

Word data and license

JP 2K teaches 2,000 Japanese words. Each word has a Japanese form, a kana reading, and an English meaning. We built that list from the sources below and publish its word fields in zentsu.app/japanese2k/icons.json.

Sources

  • The JLPT vocabulary lists by Jonathan Waller at tanos.co.uk, which are offered under the Creative Commons Attribution license.
  • The JMdict/EDICT dictionary files, the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group (EDRDG). The English meanings derive from these files, and we use them in accordance with the EDRDG license.
  • open-anki-jlpt-decks, which compiled these lists and is released under the MIT License.

The data is provided as is and can contain mistakes. If you find a wrong reading or meaning, email support@zentsu.app with the word and the fix.
