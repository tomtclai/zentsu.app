Learn Japanese: 2,000 Words

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This policy applies to Learn Japanese: 2,000 Words, also called JP 2K, published by Zentsu LLC. We keep study records on your iPhone. The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Study data on your iPhone

The app stores heard words, study progress, review dates, Library order and settings such as script display, voice and speech speed. Optional reminder settings are also stored locally. These records are not sent to Zentsu, and we cannot see them. The app does not sync study progress between devices.

The current app includes its vocabulary and pictures. It does not download a word list, icon map or pictures from our website during study. Corrections to this bundled content arrive through app updates.

Speech and Apple services

Japanese speech is generated on your iPhone using an installed iOS text-to-speech voice. The app does not use the microphone or record you. Downloading an additional voice uses Apple's services and requires an internet connection.

Apple handles app downloads, purchase and restore requests under its own terms and privacy policy. The optional JP 2K Full purchase adds access to more words. We do not receive payment-card details. These Apple services can require a network connection even though vocabulary study works offline.

Optional reminders use iPhone notifications. You can change their time or turn them off in the app, or revoke notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Older builds and website requests

Earlier builds downloaded a public picture map and picture files from zentsu.app on launch or Library refresh. We retain those files for compatibility. Those requests do not include study progress, settings or an account identifier.

As with visits to this website, the hosting provider receives ordinary request information, including the IP address and User-Agent needed to deliver and secure the site. The older app's User-Agent identifies the app. We do not use those requests to build a profile of your study activity. The Zentsu website privacy policy explains website services separately.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you choose to include. We use that correspondence to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or payment-card details.

Deleting data

Settings includes separate actions to reset heard progress and study progress. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Copies included in iCloud Backup or computer backups follow your backup settings. We hold no copy of your study records to delete.

For questions about information you have sent by email, contact privacy@zentsu.app.

Security and children

Local records rely on the security controls of your iPhone and its backups. Website connections use HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

The app does not collect study records or ask children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact privacy@zentsu.app.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. For privacy questions, email privacy@zentsu.app. For app help, visit Support.

The Zentsu terms of use apply to the app. The vocabulary has separate source credits and data license terms.
