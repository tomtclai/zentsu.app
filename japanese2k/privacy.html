Privacy policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

JP 2K support

This privacy policy applies to JP 2K (the "Application"), an iPhone app for learning Japanese vocabulary published by Zentsu LLC. Zentsu LLC is referred to below as "we," "us," or the "Service Provider." The service is provided "AS IS".

The short version: JP 2K keeps your study progress and settings on your iPhone. It works without an account, and it contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Its only network activity is downloading a public list of word pictures, and picture files, from zentsu.app. Those requests carry none of your data.

What information does the Application store?

The Application stores the words you have played, the words you answered correctly in quizzes, your Library order, and your settings, such as script display, speech speed, and voice. This information is saved in the Application's local storage on your iPhone. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it. Registration with Zentsu is not required.

What does the Application download?

When you open the Application, and when you pull down on the Library to refresh it, the Application requests a public icon map from zentsu.app/japanese2k/icons.json. If that map points to picture files, the Application downloads those files from zentsu.app. The Application stores the map and pictures on your iPhone so that corrected pictures appear without an app update.

These requests contain no progress, settings, account, device identifier, or advertising identifier. Like any web request, they reach our hosting provider with standard request information: your IP address and a User-Agent string that names the Application and its bundle identifier. Our hosting provider processes that information to deliver and secure zentsu.app. We do not use it to identify you or to build a profile of your use.

How does spoken audio work?

The Application speaks words with the text-to-speech voices built into iOS. Speech is generated on your iPhone. The Application does not use the microphone, record audio, or send the words you play anywhere.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

The Application does not collect precise information about the location of your device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Application includes no third-party SDKs and sends us no study records, so we have none to share. If you buy the optional one-time unlock, Apple processes the purchase through the App Store under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive no payment-card details from that transaction.

What are my opt-out rights and how do I delete my data?

You can delete everything the Application stores by deleting the Application through the standard uninstall process on your iPhone. Copies of Application data that are part of an iPhone backup follow the settings you chose for iCloud Backup or computer backups. Because we hold no data from the Application, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Security

The Application does not transmit your study records to us, which limits exposure through systems we operate. Information on your iPhone is protected by the security controls on that device. Downloads from zentsu.app use HTTPS. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
