This page copies what the labels print. It is not a schedule for you and it is not medical advice. Your prescriber sets your doses, and your schedule can differ from the label.

Each U.S. label for a weekly GLP-1 injection starts at a low dose and steps up in set amounts, with a minimum number of weeks at each step. The steps and the conditions for moving between them are in section 2 of the prescribing information. Below, each step is copied from the current label with its section number, beside empty columns for the date you started the step, the dose your prescriber gave you, and your notes.

Read the left column as the label's text, not as an instruction. The right columns are for what actually happened. If the two differ, the prescription is the one you follow, and the record shows the difference.

Ozempic (semaglutide) injection

Prescribing information revised 5/2026, section 2.2, Recommended Dosage.

What the label prints Date started Dose prescribed Notes
Start with 0.25 mg once weekly for 4 weeks. (2.2)
After 4 weeks on 0.25 mg, increase to 0.5 mg once weekly. (2.2)
If additional glycemic control is needed after at least 4 weeks on 0.5 mg, the dosage may be increased to 1 mg once weekly. (2.2)
If additional glycemic control is needed after at least 4 weeks on 1 mg, the dosage may be increased to 2 mg once weekly. The maximum is 2 mg once weekly. (2.2)

For type 2 diabetes with chronic kidney disease, the label lists 1 mg once weekly as the maintenance dosage, reached after at least 4 weeks on 0.5 mg (2.2).

Wegovy (semaglutide) injection

Prescribing information revised 6/2026, section 2.2, Table 1. The same label covers Wegovy tablets, which have a separate daily schedule in section 2.3 that this page does not reproduce.

What the label prints Date started Dose prescribed Notes
Weeks 1 through 4: 0.25 mg once weekly (starting dosage). (2.2, Table 1)
Weeks 5 through 8: 0.5 mg once weekly. (2.2, Table 1)
Weeks 9 through 12: 1 mg once weekly. (2.2, Table 1)
Weeks 13 through 16: 1.7 mg once weekly. (2.2, Table 1)
Week 17 onward: the maintenance dosage for the indication, listed below. (2.2, Table 1)

Maintenance dosages the label lists for each indication, all once weekly (2.2):

  • Cardiovascular risk reduction in adults: 2.4 mg (recommended) or 1.7 mg.
  • Weight reduction in adults: 1.7 mg or 2.4 mg (recommended). The label allows an increase to a maximum of 7.2 mg for patients who have tolerated 2.4 mg for at least 4 weeks, under the condition stated in section 2.2.
  • Weight reduction in pediatric patients aged 12 years and older: 2.4 mg (recommended) or 1.7 mg.
  • Noncirrhotic MASH with moderate to advanced liver fibrosis in adults: 2.4 mg, which the label allows to be decreased to 1.7 mg if 2.4 mg is not tolerated.

The label also says that if a dose is not tolerated during escalation, the prescriber can consider delaying the next step by 4 weeks (2.2).

Mounjaro (tirzepatide) injection

Prescribing information revised 8/2026, section 2.1, Recommended Dosage. The label states the steps as 2.5 mg increments. The rows below list the single-dose pen strengths from section 3, so each one is a step the label allows.

What the label prints Date started Dose prescribed Notes
Start with 2.5 mg once weekly. (2.1)
{{ strength }} mg once weekly. The label's condition for each 2.5 mg increase: additional glycemic control is needed, after at least 4 weeks on the current dose. (2.1, 3)

The maximum is 15 mg once weekly in adults and 10 mg once weekly in pediatric patients aged 10 years and older (2.1).

Zepbound (tirzepatide) injection

Prescribing information revised 8/2026, section 2.1, Recommended Dose Escalation Schedule, and section 2.2, Recommended Maintenance and Maximum Dosage.

{%- assign zepbound_steps = '7.5,10,12.5,15' | split: ',' %} {%- for strength in zepbound_steps %} {%- endfor %}
What the label prints Date started Dose prescribed Notes
Start with 2.5 mg once weekly for 4 weeks. The label says 2.5 mg is for starting treatment and is not a maintenance dosage. (2.1)
After 4 weeks, increase to 5 mg once weekly. (2.1)
{{ strength }} mg once weekly. The label allows each 2.5 mg increase after at least 4 weeks on the current dose. (2.1, 3)

Maintenance dosages the label lists, all once weekly (2.2):

  • Weight reduction and long-term maintenance: 5 mg, 10 mg, or 15 mg.
  • Obstructive sleep apnea: 10 mg or 15 mg.
  • The maximum for all indications is 15 mg.

Keeping this record in Dial

Dial does not include titration schedules and does not suggest a next dose. It records the amount you enter each time you log, so History shows the date each new amount first appears. The Dial Pro dose chart and PDF report show the same changes over time, which you can compare with a paper record like this one. The printable dose log covers the weekly entries in between.
